Finding: Exposed phpMyAdmin Database Administration Interface
Target: 192.168.77.20
Port: 80/TCP
Affected path: /phpMyAdmin/
Evidence: HTTP 200 response; phpMyAdmin interface and username/password login form are accessible.
Impact: Exposes a database administration interface to network users, increasing the attack surface and risk of unauthorized access if authentication or software security is weak.
Severity: Informational-Low (exposure confirmed; compromise not demonstrated).
Recommendation: Restrict access to trusted IPs or administrators, keep phpMyAdmin updated, and enforce strong authentication.
